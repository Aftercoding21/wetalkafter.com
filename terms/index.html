After is a quiet place to be with a loss, alongside people who have been through one too. These terms are the agreement between you and us. We’ve tried to keep them short and plain.

In short

  • After is for people aged 18 or over.
  • It’s peer support, not counselling or a crisis service. If you’re in danger, call your local emergency number.
  • Be kind. No sexual messages, no asking for money or contact details, no AI or bots.
  • Break the rules and you may be warned, suspended or banned. You can always ask us to look again.
  • What you write and keep here is yours.
  • After is free.

Who we are

After is run by [company name] OÜ, a company registered in Estonia (registry code [number]), [registered address in Estonia]. Contact: hello@wetalkafter.com.

What After is, and isn’t

After lets you keep private memories, meet in small live groups (5 people, plus 1 guest the host may invite), and talk one to one, with people you’ve met there or with someone who is around when you need to talk. Everyone here is a real person who has been through a loss. They listen as people who understand, not as professionals.

After is not a crisis service, and not medical, psychological or legal advice. We can’t watch every conversation. If you or someone else is in danger, call 999 in the UK or 112 in Ireland and France. Samaritans (116 123, UK and Ireland) and 3114 (France) are there any time. Help, in the top corner of every screen, shows these lines.

Who can use After

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • You can have one account, and it’s for you only.
  • You must give a real email address and a mobile number you can receive texts on.
  • You can’t use After if we’ve banned you before.
  • You agree to quick human checks: Cloudflare checks you’re a person when you sign up, sign in, and before you start talking (at most about once a day). If your account shows signs of being run by a program, we may ask you to check again, and a person on our team may look at it.

How we look after each other

These rules keep After safe for people at a very hard time. Please:

  • Be kind. No insults, mocking, threats, hate, or pressure on anyone to share more than they want.
  • Nothing sexual, ever, and never anything involving anyone who might be under 18.
  • No asking for money, selling, or advertising, and no asking for phone numbers, addresses or social media. Links and email addresses can’t be sent in chats.
  • No AI or bots. Write your own words. Don’t use AI to write messages to others, and never let a program, bot or AI agent use your account or sign up on your behalf.
  • Be yourself. Don’t pretend to be someone else or to have had a loss you haven’t.
  • Keep what others share private. Don’t copy, screenshot or repeat it outside After.
  • Don’t encourage anyone to harm themselves, or share ways to do it.
  • Don’t try to get round a ban, break the app, or reach information that isn’t yours.

If someone breaks the rules

You can hide someone, ask your group to remove them, report them, or, if you’re hosting the group, ban them from it. A person on our team reads every report.

  • The first time, we usually remove the messages and send a warning.
  • After that, each time brings a 7-day suspension. After three suspensions, the account is closed.
  • Some things mean an immediate ban: sexual messages to anyone who might be under 18, threats, asking for money, and using AI or a bot to reply.
  • If three different people report someone within a day, their account is paused until a person on our team has looked.

We tell the person what we decided, which rule was broken, and why. We tell the person who reported what we did, without saying who reported. If you think we got it wrong, you can ask us to look again from the app, and a person will review it.

Your content

What you write, record and keep in After is yours. You give us permission to store it and show it to the people you choose: the people in your group, the person you’re talking with, or the people you share a memory with. We use it for nothing else, and never to train AI. Please only add things you have the right to share, and be careful with photos or recordings of other people.

Group messages disappear 15 minutes after a meeting ends. Your memories stay until you delete them. Our privacy policy explains what we keep and for how long.

Your account

Keep your password to yourself. You can delete your account at any time in Settings, and everything that’s yours goes with it. We may close an account that breaks these terms, as described above.

Price

Groups, Talk, Help, the Reading corner and writing in Memories are free, always. You can also keep 50 photos, 5 videos (up to 1 minute each), 20 voice notes and 3 files (up to 50 MB each). In Calm, breathing, grounding and writing are free.

We may later offer After+, an optional subscription for unlimited photos, voice notes and files, videos up to 10 minutes (with 50 GB of space as a fair-use limit), and all of Calm (guided meditations, a music library, left-right sessions, sound journeys, body scan and stretches; everyone can try each of these once for free), at £4.99 a month or £39.99 a year in the UK, €4.99 a month or €39.99 a year in Ireland and France, and R$ 19,90 a month or R$ 159,90 a year in Brazil. Later, After+ Groups may add live voice sessions in small groups led by a grief professional (not therapy), at a price we’ll show before you subscribe. If you subscribe and later stop, you keep it until the end of the period you paid for, and you keep everything you’ve already added, even if it’s more than the free plan allows. You can still open, share and save it all. You just can’t add new photos, videos, voice notes or files until you’re back under the free limits or subscribe again. Payments go through the App Store or Google Play, under their rules for cancelling and refunds. Crisis help, groups with other people, Talk and writing are never paid, and we never sell your information.

After is new, and it will change as we learn. If we change these terms in a way that matters, we’ll tell you in the app before it takes effect. If you don’t agree, you can delete your account.
