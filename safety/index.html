Safety

A safe place to come as you are.

People come to After at some of the hardest moments of their lives. So safety isn’t an extra here: it’s built into everything, quietly, so you can simply be with people who understand.

Only real people

  • Every account is confirmed with a mobile number. One person, one account.
  • Someone who is banned can’t come back with the same number or email.
  • After is for adults: you must be 18 or older.

No AI in your conversations

  • No AI talks to you, reads your chats, or learns from what you share. Every conversation is between real people.
  • Like many small teams, we used AI tools to help design and build the app itself. Never in your conversations.

A wall against bots and AI agents

  • A quick human check, from Cloudflare, protects sign-up and the moment you start talking. Most of the time you won’t even notice it.
  • Behind it, After quietly looks for signs of a machine, never at what you write. Accounts that look automated are checked again and seen by our team.
  • No face scans, no ID, no waiting period. Just real people.

If something feels wrong

  • You can report or block anyone, in any chat or group, in two taps.
  • A real person on our team reads every report and decides what happens: a warning, a suspension, or closing the account. You’ll hear back.
  • When several people report someone, their account is paused straight away while we look.
  • Links and email addresses can’t be sent in messages, because most scams aimed at grieving people start with a link.

Groups that stay gentle

  • Small groups, of up to six people, for two hours. Every group has a host.
  • The host can ask someone to leave, and so can the group together.
  • Group messages disappear 15 minutes after the meeting ends.
  • People here listen as people who understand, not as therapists. Volunteers get clear guidance, and never act as counsellors.

Your privacy

  • You only show a username. No profile photos. Your email and number are never shown to anyone.
  • Your memories are private unless you choose to share one.
  • Our team only reads conversations that someone has reported, or when someone is worried about a person’s safety.
  • You can delete your account, and everything in it, at any time.

Help, one tap away

Every screen in After has a Help button with a free crisis line for your country. If you write something that sounds like you’re not safe, you’ll privately see where to find help straight away. And if you’re worried about someone, you can tell us.

If it’s too much right now: UK and Ireland, Samaritans 116 123 · France 3114 · free, any time. If a life is in danger, call 999 (UK) or 112 (France and Ireland).
