You’re not alone

Real people. Real stories.

Whatever you’re going through, from bereavement to a breakup, After is a place to feel understood.

Connect with people who have experienced something similar, share your story in a small group, talk one-on-one, or hold on to the memories that matter most. There’s no right way to feel, and no need to face it all alone.

Three ways to connect

Your way, at your own pace.

You’ll meet people who understand loss because they’ve experienced it too. Not as therapists or counsellors, but as people willing to listen, share their stories, and support one another.

  • Groups

    Join a live grief support group with up to six people who have experienced a similar loss. For two hours, share your story, listen to others, or simply be present. There’s no pressure to speak, and you’re always free to leave whenever you wish.

    Messages disappear 15 minutes after the meeting ends.

  • Talk

    Sometimes, one conversation makes all the difference. If you connect with someone in a group and you both wish to stay in touch, you can continue talking privately.

    Nobody can message you unless you’ve both said yes.

  • Remember

    Some memories are too precious to lose. Keep their stories alive through photos, words, voice recordings, letters, and the little things that remind you of them.

    Only you can see it, unless you choose to share one memory.

Our promise

Real people. Real conversations. Your privacy matters.

After is built around genuine human connection. No AI companions, no automated replies pretending to be people. Just real conversations with others who understand what you’re going through.

  • Your identity stays private. Use a username, without a public profile photo. Your email address and phone number are never displayed to other members.
  • Your conversations are personal. We don’t routinely read private messages. Content may be reviewed when it’s reported or flagged for safety, in accordance with our privacy and safety policies.
  • Support resources are always within reach. Find crisis and emergency support information for your country. After is a peer-support community, not an emergency service.
  • You’re free to leave. Delete your account directly in the app. Your personal data will be deleted in accordance with our privacy policy and applicable legal requirements.

Like many small teams, we used AI tools to help design and build the app itself. That never involves anything you share.

Questions

Before you come in

Is After counselling, or a crisis service?

No. After is support from people who have been through a loss too. They listen as people who understand, not as professionals. If you’re in danger, call 999 in the UK or 112 in Ireland and France. Samaritans (116 123, UK and Ireland) and 3114 (France) are there any time, and Help in the app shows them on every screen.

What kind of loss is it for?

Any loss that changed your life: a parent, a partner, a child, a brother or sister, a friend, a pet, or something that ended, like a marriage. You find groups by loss, so you’re with people in a similar place.

Who can join?

Anyone aged 18 or over. You choose a username; nobody sees your real name, email or number.

When and where does it open?

In the UK and Ireland first, then France, then Brazil, on iPhone and Android. Leave your email below and we’ll tell you once, when it opens where you live.

Who can read what I write?

The people you’re talking with, and nobody else. Group messages disappear 15 minutes after each meeting. Your memories are only yours unless you share one. A person on our team reads a conversation only if someone reports it or is worried about someone in it.

Is it really free?

Yes. Groups, Talk, Help and writing are free, always. One day there may be an optional After+ for extra storage space for photos and recordings. If there is, nothing you already have will ever be locked.
