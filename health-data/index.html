Consumer health data privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

This page is for people in Washington, Nevada, Connecticut and other US states with consumer health data laws. It adds to our Privacy policy, which says who runs After and everything else we keep.

In short

  • We never sell your health data and never use it for ads.
  • No AI reads it or learns from it.
  • We only use it to run After for you and to keep people safe.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time, and everything that’s yours goes with it.

What health data we collect

After never asks for health records, symptoms or diagnoses. But After is for people living with a loss, so some of what you share may say something about your physical or mental health:

  • Your loss, only if you choose to add it in Settings: who or what you lost, and roughly when. This can include a pregnancy loss, or a death by suicide.
  • What you write in groups, private chats and Remember (your private memories), which may mention your health, your grief or how you are coping.
  • Safety records: reports, and conversations someone flagged with “I’m worried about them”.

We don’t collect your location, your contacts, health records, or data from health apps or devices. When you write a word that may mean you’re in danger, the app shows you a crisis line on your phone. Nobody else is told, and we don’t keep a record of it.

Where it comes from

Only from you, when you type or upload it in After, and from other members when they report a message or flag a conversation.

Why we use it

  • To give you the service you asked for: matching you with people with a similar loss, groups, chats and your memories.
  • To keep people safe: reading reported or flagged conversations, and acting on them.

We ask for your consent separately, the first time you write in a group, write in Talk, or keep a memory. You can withdraw each one in Settings → What you’ve agreed to. We don’t use it for anything else.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell your health data or share it with anyone for their own use. It is only handled:

  • by Supabase, our database and file storage (servers in London, UK), which stores it on our instructions;
  • by other members, only for what you choose to post in a group or chat, or a memory you choose to share;
  • by the police or other authorities, only if the law requires it, or if we believe someone’s life is in immediate danger.

Your rights

  • See it: ask us to confirm whether we hold your health data, get a copy, and get a list of everyone we have shared it with.
  • Delete it: go to Settings → Delete my account, and everything that’s yours is removed from our active systems, including everything you wrote in chats and groups. To ask us to erase your health data, write to privacy@wetalkafter.com.
  • Withdraw your consent: at any time, in Settings → What you’ve agreed to, by deleting your account, or by emailing us.

Write to privacy@wetalkafter.com from the email address you signed up with. It’s free, we reply within 45 days, and we never treat you differently for using these rights.

Appeals. If we turn down a request, reply to our answer with “Appeal” and someone will look at it again within 45 days. If you’re still not happy, you can contact your state Attorney General: in Washington, atg.wa.gov.

Changes

If we ever want to use your health data in a new way, we’ll ask for your consent first. The date at the top shows when this page last changed.
